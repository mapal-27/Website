Family-run.
Made with heart.

Kwao (kaaow) means rice in Thai. At Kwao Thai, our family serves authentic Thai food with fragrant jasmine rice, care and a smile.

Thai meals are made for sharing: a plate of rice, dishes in the middle, and a balance of salty, sour, sweet and spicy flavors. Choose the heat that feels right for you.
